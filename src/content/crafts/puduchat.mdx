## What it is

Puduchat is an AI customer-service chatbot aimed at small and medium businesses — the ones losing sales because nobody answered the message in time. It plugs into a website and WhatsApp through a single dashboard, and learns from the business's own materials so its answers sound like the business, not like a generic assistant.

## Why I'm building it

Plenty of small shops can't justify hiring around the clock to answer the same handful of questions over and over. The pitch is straightforward: stop losing leads at 11 p.m. without growing the headcount.
