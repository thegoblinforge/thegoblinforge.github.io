I've spent fifteen-plus years at the bench — most of it on backend systems, APIs, and the distributed pieces that hold real products together. The work has happened in different shops along the way, but the trade is the same: take a vague problem, beat it into a service, ship it, and make it stop breaking at three in the morning.

These days the work I'm most proud of is the unglamorous part of the craft — mentoring, post-mortems, the patient business of turning incidents into systems that don't repeat them. I work mostly in Go and Java/Spring Boot, with a long tail of Kafka, Kubernetes, gRPC, Node, and whatever the problem actually needs.

thegoblinforge is where the after-hours work lives — the prototypes, the half-products, the things I'm still hammering on. The tools at this bench skew a little newer than the day job: Astro, React, and Express on the surface; LangChain and PydanticAI for the AI plumbing; and plenty of work done shoulder-to-shoulder with coding AI assistants.
